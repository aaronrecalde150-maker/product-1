## 1. About Me (the User)

I am a college student with a learning disability, doing homework at home, with a tight morning routine (up at 7, leave 8:00-8:15, 2-hour commute, workout on arrival around 10). After about 60-90 minutes of homework I get tired, and I take a break by going on my phone. 

**The last 3 times this happened:**

1. On Wednesday night at home, I started studying for a midterm around 10 PM with no plan, just reading my notes. I took a break near 11 and didn’t get back to studying until near 1 AM. I kept studying until I was too tired to continue, then arrived on campus at 12 instead of 10 and skipped my workout.
2. On another Wednesday, I finished class at 7:40 PM and started studying for a quiz around 8 PM at home. I took a break around 9:30 and didn’t get back to studying until 11. I studied until about 12 AM, woke up late, left late, arrived on campus at 1 PM, and missed my workout.
3. Last Monday, after class ended at 7:40 PM, I kept working on my spec at home. Around 8:30 I took a break on my phone and didn’t get back to it until close to 10 PM, when I concluded for the night and went to sleep at 11.

**What it costs me:**

- ⏱️ Time:about 1.5-2 hours per long break (from my last 3 times). I estimate 1-2 hours most days, but I’ve never measured it.
- 💸 Money:none
- 🔋 Energy:exhausted from late nights
- 🙂 Joy:stressed from missing my workouts

---

## 2. Problem Statement

**Gut check:** I need my breaks to end within 15 minutes because they have been running 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours

**Five Whys:**
1. Why do my breaks run 1.5 to 2 hours? Nothing tells me to stop, so I stop only when I see how much time has passed.
2. Why does nothing stop me? The apps keep me glued, and I lose track of time.
3. Why do I go to the phone? I get tired after 60-90 minutes and want something easy.
4. Why do I have no stopping point? I have no clear structure to follow.
5. Why? My homework is unpredictable, so I can’t break it into equal pieces the way I did with the 51 pages.

**Problem statement:**

When, after about 60-90 minutes of homework, I get tired, and I take a break by going on my phone, I struggle to stop. I only stop when I see how much time has passed. This costs me 1.5-2 hours per long break, late nights, a later wake-up, and a missed workout. This happens because my homework is unpredictable, so I don’t have a clear structure to follow, and it’s easy to get lost in my breaks. Right now I’ve tried a phone timer and a chatbot schedule. The timer didn’t work because I silenced it and went right back to scrolling. The chatbot schedule only worked for the 51 pages of notes, not for regular homework, because each problem takes an unknown amount of time.

**I'll know this is solved when:** I’ll know this is solved when my breaks end within 15 minutes, and I can be in bed by 11 PM at least 5 nights a week.

## 3. Existing Solutions

| What I use or tried | What it does well | Why it falls short *for me* |
|---|---|---|
|Phone timer|Tells me when time is up; I’ve seen it work for friends|I silence it and go right back to scrolling. It can signal, but it can’t make me stop|
|Chatbot-made schedule|Gave me clear structure (51 pages of notes, 3 sessions a day for 3 days) and my breaks dropped to about 30 minutes|It only worked because pages are equal-sized. Regular homework is unpredictable, since each problem takes an unknown amount of time, so I never used it again|

**The gap:** Neither gets me to actually end a break, and neither gives me structure for work where each problem takes an unknown amount of time.
